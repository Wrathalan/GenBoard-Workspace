# Graphic Anime Generation Prompts

Status: standing default requested by the user on 2026-09-05.
Visual direction: Gurren Lagann / Studio Trigger-inspired, with strong likeness, bold silhouettes, dramatic perspective, near-black shadows and greatly simplified two-tone forms.

## Scope and priority

Apply this direction automatically to ALL future image generations, edits, variations and generated still frames, including visual prompts prepared for other image tools. It is a standing preference; do not ask the user to confirm it for each generation. A later explicit user request takes precedence where it differs.

Use the entire visual language across characters, creatures, props, environments and scenes. The default 9:16 full-body overhead setup is the character presentation preset. Adapt it to the requested deliverable when a scene, group, object study, turnaround, cover or other format requires different framing. Preserve required geography, subject counts and functional anatomy.

For future work, this direction supersedes older shared painterly-realism style instructions. Preserve the subject's canon and the current task's requirements. Earlier generation records remain historical records.

## Prepare each prompt

State the actual subject, action, required scene, reference roles, identity constraints and any current format or camera requirements. Fill these from the user's request and supplied references before generation; never send unresolved placeholders. When no reference exists, establish these details from the brief and retain them consistently across variations.

Label each input image as an edit target, identity/design reference, style reference or composition reference. Preserve the strongest possible likeness to the designated identity reference. A style reference must not supply another subject's face, anatomy, clothing, accessories or palette.

Carry the successful simplification and deep-shadow treatment into the FIRST generation. Use the refinement prompt only if the result needs a correction.

## Universal master prompt

PRIMARY REQUEST
Create or transform [SUBJECT AND REQUIRED ACTION/STATE] in a Gurren Lagann / Studio Trigger-inspired graphic anime style while retaining the strongest possible likeness and core identity.

REFERENCE AND IDENTITY
[LIST EACH REFERENCE AND ITS ROLE, OR STATE THAT THE SUBJECT COMES FROM THE BRIEF.]
Preserve [SUBJECT-SPECIFIC IDENTITY AND DESIGN CONSTRAINTS]. Retain recognizable facial structure, apparent age, skin tone, eye and hair colors, defining anatomy, signature silhouette, costume coverage, owned equipment, material identities and essential motifs where applicable. For creatures, objects and environments, preserve their defining proportions, construction, geometry and landmark relationships. Simplify how these features are drawn while keeping them unmistakably recognizable.

POSE AND PROPORTIONS
Use a cool, powerful, confident pose with a strong line of action, clear weight distribution and dramatic presence. For humanoid character presentations, favor very tall, elegantly elongated proportions and long limbs, while respecting age, species and anatomy that define the subject. Maximize appeal through pose, silhouette, perspective and large shape groupings. Balance confident straights and crisp corners against exaggerated curves, flourish and elegant arcs.

CAMERA AND COMPOSITION
Default character presentation: 9:16 vertical frame, full body entirely visible including feet, a strong grounded stance, and a distinctly high-angle three-quarter OVERHEAD camera with dramatic foreshortening. Make the elevated viewpoint unmistakable: the nearer forms dominate and the more distant forms recede, while the pose remains tall and powerful. Arrange major appendages and accessories for an instantly readable silhouette.
Default setting: a glowing white floor against a pure black background, with subtle upward light and a clean cast shadow.
[INSERT ANY CURRENT FORMAT, CAMERA, ACTION OR SCENE REQUIREMENT.]
When the requested deliverable requires another setting, format or viewpoint, carry the same graphic hierarchy, strong perspective and clear silhouette into that composition.

LIGHTING AND VALUES
Strongly backlight the subject with an intense white key light, reinforced by subtle underlighting where the floor or scene permits. Keep the figure predominantly in flat near-black shadow; aim for roughly 80-85 percent of non-emissive body and clothing surfaces in dark masses when compatible with the subject's required readability. Reserve bright planes for the most informative facial and structural cues.
Bright, clean, continuous rim illumination wraps the silhouette through tapered illuminated surface planes INSIDE its edges. NO WHITE OUTLINE, uniform contour stroke or sticker border. Preserve dark separation where overlapping forms need it.
Use cinematic, exceptionally high-contrast, silhouette-driven lighting. Keep naturally luminous elements in broad readable light shapes and preserve the subject's established accent colors.

STYLE AND SHAPE LANGUAGE
Ultra-clean, graphical, stylish, luxurious anime key art with the bold perspective and confident shape design of Gurren Lagann / Studio Trigger. Use two-tone cel shading per material: one flat shadow tone and one flat light tone. Group shadows into large uninterrupted masses. Use sharp planar cuts, crisp corners, powerful angular construction and sweeping curves. Every silhouette must be sharper, clearer and immediately readable from a distance.

SIMPLIFICATION
Greatly simplify for animation. Merge small details into a few strong graphic masses. Group hair into large designed locks; fur, feathers or foliage into broad tiers; armor, clothing, architecture and machinery into their strongest identifying planes. Retain only details necessary for identity, expression, function or the requested story. Eliminate unnecessary micro-detail, intricate surface rendering, tiny seams, wrinkles, decorative cracks and clutter. Preserve the original clothing coverage and all essential subject-specific features.

AVOID
Photorealistic or painterly rendering, generic glossy 3D shading, soft airbrushed form modeling, gradients across subject surfaces, grain, noise, texture clutter, excessive haze, incidental particles, tiny feathers or individual hairs, unnecessary engravings, fragmented shadows, white contour outlines, accidental cropping and unreadable silhouettes. Do not add unrelated characters, props, costume elements, magic effects, text, logos or watermarks. Preserve text, logos, effects and props when the actual request or identity requires them. Do not let the style change the subject's identity.

## Universal refinement prompt

Edit [CURRENT GENERATED IMAGE]. Use [IDENTITY/DESIGN REFERENCE, IF PROVIDED] only to maintain the correct subject.

Keep the current image's successful composition, camera, pose, proportions, subject count, expression, likeness, palette, clothing coverage, essential equipment and scene relationships. Correct only [OBSERVED ISSUE].

Push the image toward the same bold Gurren Lagann / Studio Trigger-inspired graphic anime direction. Deepen the non-emissive subject into a few broad near-black shadow masses. Preserve identity with a small number of decisive light planes. Use exactly one flat light tone and one flat shadow tone per material.

Merge fine detail into larger designed shapes. Remove unnecessary seams, cracks, individual hairs, tiny feathers, fragmented plates, texture, gradients and surface clutter as applicable. Preserve the subject's defining motifs and all requested information.

Maintain intense white backlighting and subtle underlighting. Shape rim illumination as tapered light planes inside the forms. No white outline or uniform border. Keep elegant arcs against sharp straights, clean negative space and a powerful silhouette. Make the design readable at thumbnail size without changing the approved identity, pose or composition.

## Review before delivery

Check that likeness and core design survive the simplification; the requested camera and framing are present; key limbs and shapes are readable; shadows form large dark groups; light is shaped on surfaces rather than drawn as a white border; and noise or micro-detail has not returned. For the default character setup, check full-body visibility, an unmistakable overhead view, grounded feet, white floor and pure black background.

If a correction is needed, make a targeted edit and repeat the identity constraints. Save the actual prompts used with the selected result.
